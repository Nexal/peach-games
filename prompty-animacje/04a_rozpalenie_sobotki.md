# Scene 4a: Goal I — Lighting the Great Pyre (Wielka Sobótka)

## Prompt

A massive unlit pyre stands in the center of a forest clearing — a towering structure of stacked logs and branches, built like a primitive monument reaching toward the sky. Three figures representing the three clans approach from three different directions, each carrying a single flaming torch. The torches burn in three distinct colors: gold, green, and purple. As they thrust their torches into the base of the pyre simultaneously, the fire explodes upward in a spiraling column of brilliant white-orange flame, illuminating the entire clearing and the upturned faces of a gathered crowd. Sparks and embers fly upward into the night sky, merging with the stars. The camera starts at ground level looking up at the pyre, then slowly tilts back to take in the full towering height of the burning pyre against the full moon. The heat shimmer is visible in the air. Photorealistic, awe-inspiring scale, cinematic wide angle, epic and primordial.

**Music and sound:** Full crescendo — the complete Slavic choir now singing with power, deep frame drums pounding a steady ritual rhythm, a primal horn blasting a triumphant call. The sound of crackling fire is loud and immersive. This is the moment of unity — three becoming one. The music swells to a peak as the pyre ignites, then holds on a sustained chord of pure awe.

## Notes

- Duration: 6-8 seconds
- Camera: ground level looking up, slow tilt back to full height
- Style: photorealistic, epic scale, warm firelight palette
- This is the shared goal — all clans must achieve this together
