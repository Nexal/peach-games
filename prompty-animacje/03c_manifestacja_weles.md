# Scene 3c: Clan Manifestation — Weles

## Prompt

From complete darkness, a static clan emblem image ([REFERENCE_IMAGE_WELES_CLAN_EMBLEM]) emerges slowly into view, as if summoned from the shadows. The emblem is a flat, pre-existing PNG image — it does not change shape or animate internally. Purple runes ignite one by one in the air around the emblem, orbiting it in a slow spiral, each flaring violet then settling into a steady glow. Low purple fog seeps in from the edges of the frame, creeping along the bottom and curling around the emblem without obscuring it. A spectral serpent of violet smoke coils once around the emblem's perimeter, passing behind it, then dissipates into floating embers. The camera starts in extreme close-up on the center of the emblem, then slowly pulls back to reveal the full symbol against a dark, twisted forest backdrop, purple mist and glowing runes drifting in the air around it. Photorealistic, slow-motion, cinematic, mysterious and seductive. The emblem itself remains crisp and static — all motion is in the surrounding effects, light, fog, and particles.

**Music and sound:** A scratching, carving sound as the first rune ignites. A whispering hiss as the smoke serpent emerges. A deep, dissonant cello note sustained beneath. A single whispered word — barely audible — in an ancient-sounding tongue. No percussion. Pure mystery.

**Voiceover (Polish):** "Weles wybrał ciebie. Jesteś jego cieniem, jego wiedzą, jego championem. Nie zawiedź go."
