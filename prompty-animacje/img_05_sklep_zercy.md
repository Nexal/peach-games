# Image Prompt 5: The Żerca's Shop (Sklep Żercy)

## Prompt

Photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed, hyper-detailed textures. A dark alcove between two ancient gnarled trees. A crooked wooden stall flickering with candlelight. The Żerca — a hooded figure, face completely obscured in shadow, only gnarled hands with rings visible — gesturing over a collection of mystical items on weathered cloth. The items, left to right: a glowing amber potion pulsing like a heartbeat, a black feather dripping living shadow, a wooden charm carved with spiraling runes, and a small glass vial containing swirling golden smoke pressing against the glass. Each item glows with its own inner light — amber, shadow-purple, rune-white, gold. Rich textures — rough-hewn wood, dripping candle wax, wisps of incense smoke curling upward, bark and moss on surrounding trees. Warm amber and deep shadow palette. The feeling of stumbling upon something forbidden and desirable. Dark fantasy marketplace.

## Notes

- Composition: wide shot of the stall, items laid out left to right, Żerca behind
- Each item has its own colored glow
- Style: photorealistic, hyper-detailed, dark fantasy marketplace
