# Scene 5: The Żerca's Shop (Sklep Żercy)

## Prompt

In a dark alcove between two ancient, gnarled trees, a crooked wooden stall flickers with candlelight. The Żerca — a hooded figure whose face is completely obscured in shadow, only gnarled, ring-covered hands visible — gestures slowly over a collection of mystical items laid out on weathered cloth. The items, left to right: a glowing amber potion that pulses like a heartbeat, a black feather that drips living shadow onto the cloth, a wooden charm carved with spiraling runes that faintly rotates on its own, and a small glass vial containing swirling golden smoke that presses against the glass like a trapped spirit. As the camera glides smoothly across the stall from left to right, each item briefly flares with its own inner light in sequence — amber, shadow-purple, rune-white, gold — as if responding to the camera's gaze. The scene is rich with texture — rough-hewn wood, dripping candle wax, wisps of incense smoke curling upward, bark and moss on the surrounding trees. Photorealistic, hyper-detailed, dark fantasy marketplace, warm amber and deep shadow palette, the feeling of stumbling upon something forbidden and desirable.

**Music and sound:** A mysterious, quiet motif — the sound of coins or small stones being counted, clicking softly. A low, nasal instrument playing a meandering, serpent-like melody — like a duduk or a hurdy-gurdy in a minor key. A whispered incantation beneath it all, words unclear but ancient. The candle flames hiss softly. When each item flares, a subtle tonal shimmer matches its color — warm amber chime, deep purple hum, crystalline ring, golden swell.

## Notes

- Duration: 4-5 seconds
- Camera: smooth left-to-right glide across the stall
- Style: photorealistic, hyper-detailed, dark fantasy marketplace
- Each item flares in sequence as camera passes
