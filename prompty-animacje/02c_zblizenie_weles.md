# Scene 2c: Close-up — Weles

## Prompt

Extreme close-up on the face of WELES, the Slavic god of magic and the underworld. The face is [REFERENCE_IMAGE_WELES]. His expression is cunning, knowing, slightly amused — as if he already sees how this ends and finds it entertaining. One eyebrow slightly raised. Purple runes reflected in his dark eyes, slowly rotating like celestial bodies. Shadows move across his face like living smoke, half-obscuring then revealing his features in a slow rhythm. His lips curl into a subtle smirk. Behind him, complete darkness with floating purple glyphs drifting like fireflies. Camera holds still. Photorealistic, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, reminiscent of Caravaggio paintings. Only half his face lit at any moment.
