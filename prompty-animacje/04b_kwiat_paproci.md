# Scene 4b: Goal II — The Fern Flower (Kwiat Paproci)

## Prompt

Deep in the darkest part of the forest, where no moonlight reaches, a single fern begins to glow from within. From its center, a luminous flower blooms in accelerated time-lapse — not a natural flower, but a crystalline, ethereal blossom made of pure shifting light, cycling slowly between gold, emerald, and violet hues. The flower detaches from the fern and floats upward, hovering at eye level, casting intricate shadow-patterns of leaves and ancient runes onto the surrounding tree trunks. A hand reaches from below the frame and gently cups the flower. As the hand touches it, the light intensifies dramatically — a flash of brilliance — then stabilizes into a steady, warm glow emanating from between the fingers. The camera is in extreme close-up on the flower initially, then slowly pulls back to reveal the dark forest around it, now softly illuminated by the flower's radiance as if the night itself has been gentled. Photorealistic, magical realism, reminiscent of macro nature photography with supernatural elements.

**Music and sound:** Near silence at first — just the faintest rustle of leaves. As the flower blooms, a single high crystalline tone rings out, pure and bell-like, resonating and fading slowly. When the hand touches the flower, a full chord blooms — warm, golden, triumphant but intimate — like a music box opening in a cathedral. The sound of held breath. Then a quiet, reverent stillness.

## Notes

- Duration: 5-7 seconds
- Camera: extreme close-up on flower, slow pullback to forest
- Style: photorealistic, magical realism, shifting tri-color glow
- This is the competitive goal — only one clan wins this
