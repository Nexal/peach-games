# Scene 3b: Clan Manifestation — Mokosz

## Prompt

From complete darkness, a static clan emblem image ([REFERENCE_IMAGE_MOKOSZ_CLAN_EMBLEM]) emerges slowly into view, as if growing from the earth itself. The emblem is a flat, pre-existing PNG image — it does not change shape or animate internally. Green luminescence begins to pulse from behind the emblem, glowing through and around its edges like bioluminescent sap. Soft green fireflies drift upward around the emblem, leaving gentle trailing light. Moss and small vines creep into frame at the edges, framing the emblem organically but never covering it. Dewdrops materialize and cling to the emblem's surface, catching the green glow like tiny jewels. The camera starts in extreme close-up on the center of the emblem, then slowly pulls back to reveal the full symbol against a dark, mossy forest backdrop, green spores and fireflies floating in the air around it. Photorealistic, slow-motion, cinematic. The emblem itself remains crisp and static — all motion is in the surrounding effects, light, and organic particles.

**Music and sound:** The sound of growing — a subtle crackling of wood and rustling of leaves as the emblem emerges. A warm, earthy hum like a cello sustained note. Then a gentle female choir, three voices harmonizing in a wordless major chord — nurturing and ancient.

**Voiceover (Polish):** "Mokosz wybrała ciebie. Jesteś jej korzeniem, jej ziarnem, jej championem. Nie zawiedź jej."
