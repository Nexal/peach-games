# Image Prompt 4b: Goal II — The Fern Flower (Kwiat Paproci)

## Prompt

Photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed, macro photography with supernatural elements. Deep in the darkest part of the forest where no moonlight reaches. A single fern glows from within. From its center, a luminous crystalline flower made of pure shifting light — cycling between gold, emerald, and violet hues. The flower hovers at eye level, detached from the fern, casting intricate shadow-patterns of leaves and ancient runes onto the surrounding tree trunks. A hand reaches from below the frame, fingers gently cupping the flower. The light from the flower illuminates the hand and the immediate forest around it, softening the darkness. Extreme close-up on the flower with the hand, shallow depth of field, the dark forest visible but out of focus behind. Magical realism, ethereal, tri-color glow as the focal light source.

## Notes

- Composition: macro close-up, flower and hand as focal point, dark forest background
- Flower shifts between gold, green, and violet
- Style: photorealistic, magical realism, macro photography
