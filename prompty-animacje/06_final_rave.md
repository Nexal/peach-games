# Scene 6: Final Rave — Celebration of the Victorious God

## Prompt

The Great Pyre still burns at the center of the forest clearing, now a steady, powerful bonfire casting dancing light across the scene. Around it, silhouetted figures dance and celebrate — arms raised, heads thrown back in ecstasy, bodies moving in primal rhythm. The fire casts long, living shadows onto the surrounding ancient trees. Above the fire, in the rising column of smoke and sparks, a spectral vision shimmers — the victorious god's face ([REFERENCE_IMAGE_OF_WINNING_GOD]) materializes briefly in the flames and smoke, smiling with ancient, satisfied power, then dissolves back into embers and rises skyward. The scene is primal, ecstatic, ritualistic — not a modern nightclub but an ancient Slavic celebration under the open sky. Faces illuminated by firelight, sweat glistening on skin, bodies moving rhythmically. Instead of modern glowsticks, celebrants hold burning bundles of dried herbs that trail fragrant smoke and sparks. The camera circles the fire at shoulder height, weaving through the celebrating crowd, passing close to laughing, joyful, fire-lit faces, some with eyes closed in trance. Photorealistic, hand-held camera feel, warm firelight palette with deep surrounding shadows, transcendence and release.

**Music and sound:** Tribal techno — but organic, not electronic-sounding. Live frame drums driving a relentless, pounding rhythm. A dark folk melody woven through it on a wooden flute and Jew's harp, the melody ancient and modal. Throat singing — Slavic style, not Tuvan — providing a bass drone beneath the beat. The sound of the fire crackling is mixed into the rhythm as a percussive element. As the god's face appears in the smoke, the music peaks — all instruments hit together in a moment of pure catharsis — then the beat continues, driving, relentless, ecstatic. Style reference: Heilung meets dark tribal techno, Be Svendsen at Burning Man, ancient and modern fused. This is not EDM. This is ritual.
