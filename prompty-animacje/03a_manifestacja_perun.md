# Scene 3a: Clan Manifestation — Perun

## Prompt

From complete darkness, a static clan emblem image ([REFERENCE_IMAGE_PERUN_CLAN_EMBLEM]) emerges slowly into view, as if summoned from the void. The emblem is a flat, pre-existing PNG image — it does not change shape or animate internally. Around it, golden lightning cracks to life, framing the emblem with raw electric energy. Sparks cascade downward from the edges of the emblem. A warm golden aura pulses behind it like a heartbeat — dim, bright, dim, bright — growing stronger with each pulse until the emblem is fully illuminated. The camera starts in extreme close-up on the center of the emblem, then slowly pulls back to reveal the full symbol standing against a dark forest backdrop, golden embers drifting in the air around it. Photorealistic, slow-motion, cinematic. The emblem itself remains crisp and static — all motion is in the surrounding effects, light, and particles.

**Music and sound:** A thunderous boom as the emblem first appears, followed by the crackle of electricity. A deep, heroic drumbeat — single strike of a large leather drum. Then a rising heroic horn note, primal and proud.

**Voiceover (Polish):** "Perun wybrał ciebie. Jesteś jego piorunem, jego gniewem, jego championem. Nie zawiedź go."

## Notes

- Duration: 5-7 seconds
- Camera: extreme close-up on emblem center, slow pullback to full reveal
- The emblem PNG is a static reference image — do not animate or alter it
- All animation is in surrounding effects: lightning, sparks, golden aura pulse, embers
- Style: photorealistic, slow-motion, warm golden palette
