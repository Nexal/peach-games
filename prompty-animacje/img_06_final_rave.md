# Image Prompt 6: Final Rave — Celebration of the Victorious God

## Prompt

Photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed. The Great Pyre burns at the center of a forest clearing — a powerful bonfire casting dancing light across the scene. Around it, silhouetted figures celebrate — arms raised, heads thrown back in ecstasy, bodies in primal motion. In the rising column of smoke and sparks above the fire, a spectral face materializes — the victorious god ([REFERENCE_IMAGE_OF_WINNING_GOD]) — smiling with ancient satisfied power, formed from smoke and embers, dissolving upward. The celebration is primal, ritualistic — an ancient Slavic celebration under the open sky, not a modern club. Faces illuminated by firelight, sweat glistening on skin. Celebrants hold burning bundles of dried herbs trailing fragrant smoke and sparks instead of modern glowsticks. Long living shadows cast onto surrounding ancient trees. Shot at shoulder height within the crowd, fire at center, faces of celebrants visible in foreground and midground. Warm firelight palette with deep surrounding shadows. Transcendence, release, primal joy.
