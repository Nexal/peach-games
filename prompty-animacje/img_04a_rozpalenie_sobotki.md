# Image Prompt 4a: Goal I — Lighting the Great Pyre (Wielka Sobótka)

## Prompt

Photorealistic, 8k, highly detailed, epic scale. A massive pyre in the center of a forest clearing — a towering structure of stacked logs and branches built like a primitive monument. The pyre has just ignited — a spiraling column of brilliant white-orange flame explodes upward from the base. Three torches in gold, green, and purple are visible at the base, just withdrawn. Sparks and embers fly upward into the night sky, merging with the stars. A full moon above. Three silhouetted figures representing the three clans stand around the pyre, faces upturned, illuminated by the fire. The surrounding ancient trees are lit by the firelight, long dancing shadows cast outward. Heat shimmer visible in the air. Shot from a low angle looking up at the pyre, emphasizing its towering height against the moon. Warm firelight palette with deep surrounding darkness. Awe-inspiring, primordial, the moment of unity.

## Notes

- Composition: low angle, pyre as central towering element, moon above
- Three clan torch colors visible at base: gold, green, purple
- Style: photorealistic, epic scale, warm firelight
