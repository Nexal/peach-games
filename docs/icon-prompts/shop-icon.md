# Prompt dla generatora obrazów - Ikona "Sklep" (Shop)

## Wersja pełna

Icon design for "Sklep" (Shop) tab in a dark Slavic mythology game app called "Noc Kupały" (Midsummer Night).

Requirements:
- Style: Dark, mystical, with subtle neon glow effects
- Colors: Deep purple (#8A2BE2), gold (#FFD700), green (#2E8B57), black/dark background
- Subject: A stylized potion bottle, alchemical flask, or mystical store element - representing buffs, curses, and clan point transactions. Could incorporate a bubbling potion, runic symbols, or a scales/balance motif
- The icon should convey: shop, store, potions, buffs, curses, alchemy, trade
- Aesthetic: Glassmorphism, neon accents, dark Slavic rave theme
- Reference: Players will buy buffs/curses with clan points in this shop

Technical specs:
- Format: Square icon, minimal detail for small sizes
- Background: Transparent
- Colors limited to: gold (#FFD700), purple (#8A2BE2), green (#2E8B57), white/light accents on dark or transparent bg
- Simple enough to read at 24x24px but interesting at larger sizes

DO NOT include text. Only the icon graphic.
