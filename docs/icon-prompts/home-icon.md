# Prompt dla generatora obrazów - Ikona "Home" (Strona Główna)

## Wersja pełna

Icon design for "Home" (Strona Główna) tab in a dark Slavic mythology game app called "Noc Kupały" (Midsummer Night).

Requirements:
- Style: Dark, mystical, with subtle neon glow effects
- Colors: Deep purple (#8A2BE2), gold (#FFD700), green (#2E8B57), black/dark background
- Subject: A stylized Slavic rune symbol combined with a house/shelter motif - representing the "home base" concept. Could incorporate runic patterns, a sacred fire element (Kupała bonfire), or a glowing sun/rune hybrid
- The icon should convey: home base, sanctuary, starting point, Slavic mysticism
- Aesthetic: Glassmorphism, neon accents, dark Slavic rave theme
- Reference: Noc Kupały is a Slavic midsummer festival with bonfires, rituals, and sun symbolism

Technical specs:
- Format: Square icon, minimal detail for small sizes
- Background: Transparent
- Colors limited to: gold (#FFD700), purple (#8A2BE2), green (#2E8B57), white/light accents on dark or transparent bg
- Simple enough to read at 24x24px but interesting at larger sizes

DO NOT include text. Only the icon graphic.
