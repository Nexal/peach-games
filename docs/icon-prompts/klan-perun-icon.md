# Prompt dla generatora obrazów - Ikona "Klan Peruna"

## Wersja pełna

Icon design for "Klan Peruna" (Clan of Perun) in a dark Slavic mythology game app called "Noc Kupały" (Midsummer Night).

Requirements:
- Style: Dark, mystical, with subtle neon glow effects
- Primary color: Gold (#FFD700) representing lightning and fire
- Subject: A stylized lightning bolt or eagle silhouette combined with a runic symbol. The icon should represent power, courage, and divine fire of the thunder god Perun
- The icon should convey: clan identity, Perun's power, lightning, fire, strength, courage
- Aesthetic: Glassmorphism, neon glows, dark Slavic rave theme
- Reference: Perun is the Slavic god of thunder, lightning, and oak trees. His clan values strength and determination.

Technical specs:
- Format: Square icon, minimal detail for small sizes
- Background: Transparent
- Colors limited to: gold (#FFD700), red (#E74C3C), white/light accents on dark or transparent bg
- Simple enough to read at 24x24px but interesting at larger sizes

DO NOT include text. Only the icon graphic.
