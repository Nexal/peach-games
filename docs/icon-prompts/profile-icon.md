# Prompt dla generatora obrazów - Ikona "Profil" (Profile)

## Wersja pełna

Icon design for "Profil" (Profile) tab in a dark Slavic mythology game app called "Noc Kupały" (Midsummer Night).

Requirements:
- Style: Dark, mystical, with subtle neon glow effects
- Colors: Deep purple (#8A2BE2), gold (#FFD700), green (#2E8B57), black/dark background
- Subject: A stylized user/person icon with Slavic elements - could be a warrior silhouette, a hooded figure, or a stylized avatar with runic crown/headpiece. Should convey player identity and clan membership
- The icon should convey: profile, player identity, clan membership, personal stats
- Aesthetic: Glassmorphism, neon accents, dark Slavic rave theme
- Reference: Shows player info, clan, clan points, inventory

Technical specs:
- Format: Square icon, minimal detail for small sizes
- Background: Transparent
- Colors limited to: gold (#FFD700), purple (#8A2BE2), green (#2E8B57), white/light accents on dark or transparent bg
- Simple enough to read at 24x24px but interesting at larger sizes

DO NOT include text. Only the icon graphic.
